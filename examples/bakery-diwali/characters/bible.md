# Character bible

Gulmohar Bakery's Diwali reel (an example: the people and the bakery are made up). Each section starts with the
screenplay's block word for word, then goes deeper.

## RANI: Rani

**Prompt block (verbatim from screenplay.json):**

> RANI (34): Indian woman, 34, warm medium-brown skin, round face, a small dark mole above the left corner of her lip, black hair in a low loose bun with a few strands free at the temples, thin arched eyebrows, dark brown eyes, small gold stud earrings, a thin red-and-gold thread bracelet on her right wrist. Average build, about 5 ft 3 in. Wardrobe (whole film): mustard-yellow cotton kurta with a plain round neck and three-quarter sleeves, white cotton churidar, no dupatta, barefoot at home.

- Face: round face, thin arched eyebrows, dark brown eyes, a small dark mole above the left corner of her lip.
- Skin: warm medium-brown, natural texture, faint smile lines.
- Hair: black, in a low loose bun, a few strands free at the temples.
- Build: average build, about 5 ft 3 in, relaxed posture.
- Wardrobe per scene: every scene, a mustard-yellow cotton kurta (plain round neck, three-quarter sleeves), white cotton churidar, no dupatta, barefoot at home.
- Fixed details: the mole above the left lip corner, small gold studs, the red-and-gold thread bracelet on the RIGHT wrist in every close-up.
- Never: a dupatta, a watch, rings, printed fabric, text on clothes.
- Real person: no.
