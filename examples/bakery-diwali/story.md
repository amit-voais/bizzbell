# Story

## Directions

1. **Ghar jaisa (chosen):** a busy mother has five days left and no gift ready; she finds a hand-made cookie box, and
   her neighbour's first bite says "like home".
2. **The baker's hands:** a quiet, close film of the baker's hands making and packing the box, ending on the offer.
3. **Same box every year:** a family groans at the same sweet box again; this year someone brings something different.

## Chosen

Direction 1. The research says the audience's pain is no time before Diwali and a wish to give something that feels
home-made (C5, from S2). A person they recognise, with that exact problem, solved in one evening, fits a 20 s reel
better than a product-only film (direction 2) and avoids knocking other shops (direction 3).

## Beats

| Beat | Time | What happens | Voice-over idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-2 s | Rani counts on her fingers, worried | only five days to Diwali |
| Problem | 2-6 s | an empty tray in the cupboard; she thinks, picks up her phone | no gift box ready |
| Turn | 6-10 s | a baker's hands fill and tie the Diwali box | she found the hand-made kaju-pista box |
| Proof | 10-14 s | her neighbour Sunita takes a bite at the door, smiles | "like home" |
| Offer | 14-17 s | product card | 12 cookies, Rs 499 |
| Call to action | 17-20 s | end card | order today, link in bio |

The product is seen being made by hand (S04, S05) and eaten (S06). Claims used: C1, C2, C3.
