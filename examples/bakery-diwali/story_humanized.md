# Humanized story

## Tone

Rani is telling a friend about her Diwali, half laughing at herself. Casual Hinglish, short phrases, small pauses.
Not an announcer and not an ad read. Fillers where a person would really say them: arre yaar at the start, umm when
she is stuck, matlab when she explains, haan and na in the neighbour's reply.

## Voice-over

- L01: Arre yaar, Diwali mein bas paanch din bache hain...
- L02: Aur gift ka ek bhi dabba ready nahi. Umm...
- L03: Phir mila Gulmohar Bakery ka box, matlab haath se bani cookies.
- L04: Padosan boli, haan, yeh toh ghar jaisa hai na!
- L05: Barah cookies, chaar sau ninyanve rupaye.
- L06: Aaj hi order karo, link bio mein.

## Notes

- 51 words over 19 s: about 2.7 words per second; Hinglish words are short, so it is comfortable. Cut a word
  before adding one.
- "Gulmohar" is said gul-mo-har; the Devanagari spelling in screenplay.json lines[].tts carries it.
- Every claim is from story.md: hand-made, 12 cookies, Rs 499 (research C1-C3). The card (G08) shows
  "kaju-pista"; the voice keeps to the three must-say facts.
