# Character bible

One section per character and per location, headed with its screenplay.json bible id. Each section starts with
the screenplay's block **word for word** (the prompt block), then goes deeper. The sheet and plate prompts are built
from these sections.

## (ID): (name)

**Prompt block (verbatim from screenplay.json):**

> (the exact text of bibles.characters.(ID).text)

- Face: (shape, eyes, nose, lips, marks)
- Skin: (tone, texture, freckles, lines)
- Hair: (colour, length, style, parting, loose strands)
- Build: (height, frame, posture)
- Wardrobe per scene: (scene: every garment, colour, fabric, footwear)
- Fixed details: (ring finger and hand, watch wrist, glasses, bindi; what must show in every close-up)
- Never: (things the image model tends to add that must not appear)
- Real person: no / yes, CONSENT.md dated (YYYY-MM-DD), photos: refs/(files)

## (LOCATION ID): (name)

> (the exact text of bibles.locations.(ID).text)

- Light per time of day: (source and side)
- Never: (signs with words, logos, screens with content, number plates)
